This is a short English wiki for **Oboro no Ichi**, Steam AppID **4559930**, built as a static site for **oboronoichi.wiki**.

The pages that describe the game are the [guide](/guide), the [market](/market) note, the [build](/build) note, and the [stalls](/stalls) note. Each one quotes the Steam store page, the appdetails payload from 7 November 2026, or the 13 May 2026 devlog. A figure appears only when one of those sources printed it.

## What “checked” means

<Callout kind="note" title="A read date, not a playtest">
“Checked 7 Nov 2026” means the Steam API, the English store page, and the linked announcement were read that day. It does not mean someone played the Early Access build. The game was still marked coming soon. The free demo (app 5271720) was already listed as released, and this site has not played that either.
</Callout>

The support email on the Steam store page is thelabaofficial@gmail.com. The support URL field was empty, and the website field was null. That inbox belongs to TheLaba, not to this wiki.

Legal text lives on [Disclaimer](/disclaimer). Tracking lives on [Privacy](/privacy). The footer repeats both in short form.

## Images

Library hero, library capsule, hero capsule, the store header, the capsules, and the screenshots are official Steam store assets for AppID 4559930, downloaded from the Steam CDN and served from this site so pages do not hotlink Steam. Paths and source URLs are in [ATTRIBUTION.txt](/images/official/steam/ATTRIBUTION.txt). The favicon is an original lantern drawn for this site. It is not the Steam client icon.

## What is still unpublished

- A numeric Steam price. `is_free` is false and `package_groups` was empty on 7 November 2026. Early Access is described as discounted, with no amount.
- A named stall list, any recipe, or any yield.
- A market-hour schedule. “Market hours” appears only as a full-version plan.
- A controller category. The payload’s controller list was empty.
- An achievements count. The payload had no achievements block.
- Anything inside the demo or the trailers that the written store text does not also say.
