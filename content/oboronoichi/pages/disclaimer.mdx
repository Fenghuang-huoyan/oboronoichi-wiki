This site does not sell Oboro no Ichi, process purchases, redeem codes, or recover accounts. It is not responsible for purchase decisions, for Early Access changes, or for balance and content that shift after a pre-release post. The planned Steam unlock cited here is 9 November 2026, and on 7 November 2026 the store still marked the game coming soon.

Nothing here is account support. The Steam store page lists the email thelabaofficial@gmail.com and does not list a support URL. That mailbox is TheLaba’s. Mail sent there does not reach the people who edit this wiki.

The guide quotes dated official text. Where the store has not named a stall, a recipe, a price, or a code, the page says so instead of filling the gap. Steam store artwork is used to illustrate those pages and is credited in the attribution file next to the images. The site’s favicon is original and is not a TheLaba or Valve logo.

Steam user tags, Early Access answers, and screenshots can change without notice. A “checked” date is the day the source was read, not a promise that the sentence is still true in the current client.

The privacy note for this static site is on [Privacy](/privacy). A shorter copy of this disclaimer is in the footer of every page.
